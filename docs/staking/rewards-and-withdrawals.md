# Rewards & withdrawals

## Claimable rewards

The Stake page shows **claimable rewards** — fee share accumulated in supported reward tokens (may include USDC, MON, or others depending on vault configuration).

### Claiming

When rewards are available:

1. Review the claimable amount on the Stake page
2. Click the claim action
3. Confirm in your wallet

Rewards go to your connected wallet.

Reward rate is not fixed. Higher overall trading volume across Mondalore markets generally means more fees entering the vault.

## Withdrawing staked MONDO

After a deposit’s **lock period** ends:

1. Enter withdraw amount (or max available unlocked balance)
2. Confirm **Withdraw**
3. sMONDO is burned and MONDO returns to your wallet

You can only withdraw what is **unlocked**. If you made multiple deposits on different days, each unlocks on its own schedule.

## Topping up

Adding more MONDO while already staked:

- Mints more sMONDO
- Starts a **new lock** on the new deposit only
- Does not extend locks on older deposits

Plan deposits if you may need liquidity soon.

## Position stats

The Stake page also shows:

- **Total staked (protocol)** — all MONDO in the vault
- **Your stake** — your sMONDO balance
- **Current epoch** — vault accounting period
- **Exchange rate** — 1:1 MONDO/sMONDO

## Strategy notes

| Approach | Consideration |
|----------|----------------|
| Single large stake | One lock timer; simpler tracking |
| Regular small stakes | Staggered unlocks; smoother liquidity |
| Stake and forget | Claim rewards periodically; withdraw after unlock |

## Relationship to trading

Staking and trading are independent:

- Trade USDC/MON on markets for prediction exposure
- Stake MONDO for protocol fee share

Many participants do both.
