# Staking

Staking on Mondalore lets you deposit **MONDO** and receive **sMONDO** receipts while earning a slice of protocol trading fees.

## Why stake?

Every market trade pays **1.2%** to the protocol. Part of that flow is distributed to MONDO stakers who align with the platform long term.

Staking is separate from trading prediction markets — you can do either or both.

## High-level flow

1. Connect wallet with MONDO balance
2. Enter amount on the **Stake** page
3. Confirm stake transaction
4. Receive sMONDO **1:1** with deposited MONDO
5. Accrue claimable rewards over time
6. Withdraw after each deposit’s lock period ends

## What you need

- MONDO in your wallet
- Gas for stake, claim, and withdraw transactions
- Patience for the **minimum lock** on each deposit

## sMONDO

sMONDO is a **non-transferable receipt** representing your staked MONDO in the vault. It is not a tradeable token on secondary markets in the standard flow — it tracks your stake balance inside the protocol.

Exchange rate displayed: **1 MONDO = 1 sMONDO**.
