# Connect your wallet

Connecting a wallet links Mondalore to the address that holds your funds and signs transactions.

## How to connect

1. Click **Connect wallet** (or **Sign up to trade** on a market).
2. Choose your wallet provider from the list.
3. Approve the connection request in your wallet extension or mobile app.
4. If prompted, switch to the correct network — Mondalore only works on its deployed network. The app will tell you if you are on the wrong network.

## What connecting does — and does not do

**Connecting does:**

- Let the app read your wallet address and balances.
- Allow you to sign trades, claims, and other actions.

**Connecting does not:**

- Give Mondalore custody of your funds. Tokens stay in your wallet until you approve a specific transaction.
- Require sharing a password with Mondalore. Your wallet handles authentication.

## Signing in

After connecting, you may be asked to **sign a message**. This proves you control the address without spending gas. It is a standard way Web3 apps confirm identity.

## Wrong network

If you see a message about the wrong network, open your wallet and switch to the network Mondalore expects. Trades and claims will fail until you are on the correct chain.

## Security tips

- Only connect on the official Mondalore site.
- Read every transaction in your wallet before approving — check the amount and action.
- Never share your wallet’s secret recovery phrase with anyone, including support impersonators.
