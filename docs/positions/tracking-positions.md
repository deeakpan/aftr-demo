# Tracking your positions

The **Trades** page aggregates all your market exposure in one place.

## What each card shows

- **Market title** — click to open the full market page
- **Type** — Event or Price
- **Status** — whether trading is open, closed, or settled
- **Your outcomes** — which sides you hold and how many shares
- **Chance** — current implied probability for your outcome(s)
- **Pool size** — total value locked in the market

## Multiple outcomes in one market

If you traded more than one outcome in the same market (unusual but possible), each outcome appears with its own balance.

## Settled markets

Once settled, the card highlights the **winning outcome**. If you hold winning shares, a **Claim Winnings** section appears with:

- Your share balance
- Estimated payout in the market’s collateral
- A button to claim

If you already claimed, the card shows a settled summary with invested vs redeemed amounts where available.

## Refreshing

Positions update after transactions confirm. If you do not see a recent trade, wait for confirmation and pull to refresh or revisit the page.

## Indexed history

For settled markets, the app may show how much collateral you put in over the life of the market versus what you redeemed. This helps you review performance without external spreadsheets.

## Tips

- Use Trades as your checklist before **resolve after** times — know what you still hold.
- Open the market detail page for order books and charts on any position.
- Disconnecting your wallet does not close positions; shares remain on-chain.
