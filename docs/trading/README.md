# Trading

Trading on Mondalore means buying **outcome shares** — tokens that represent a bet on one specific result of a market question.

## Two ways to trade

| Method | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| **Market trade** | Entering a position immediately at the current pool price |
| **Limit order** | Setting a specific price and waiting for a match (buy or sell) |

Most new users start with a market trade. Limit orders are useful when you want a better entry, or when you want to **sell shares you already own** before settlement.

## The trade in one sentence

You spend collateral → receive outcome shares for your chosen result → if that result wins at settlement, redeem shares for payout.

## Before you trade, check

1. **Which outcome** you believe will win.
2. **Which collateral** the market uses (USDC or MON).
3. **Whether trading is still open** — markets close new positions at **stake end**, not at final settlement.
4. **The estimated shares and fees** shown in the trade panel.

## Core concepts

- **Probability** — shown as a percentage per outcome; reflects how much collateral is on each side of the pool.
- **Shares** — your position size; more shares mean a larger payout if you win (relative to the pool).
- **Fees** — each market trade charges 1.5% of the amount you put in. See [Fees & slippage](fees-and-slippage.md).
