# Zedkr Market

Zedkr Market is a prediction market app focused on fast market participation and clear outcome ownership.

Users sign in with Para, explore live market categories, and take positions on outcomes. Each position is represented by transferable outcome shares that can be held, traded, or redeemed after settlement.

## What Users Can Do

- Sign in with Para (email, phone, or social) — no MetaMask required for the session.
- Set a display name to participate in market activity.
- Browse markets by category (Trending, Newest, Crypto, Politics, Finance, Tech, Economy, Sports, Gaming, Breaking).
- View available balance and prepare deposits.
- Navigate market features quickly on desktop and mobile.

## How Zedkr Market Works (User View)

- Markets are created with clear outcomes and an expiry time.
- You choose an outcome and deposit collateral to receive outcome shares.
- If your outcome wins at settlement, your shares can be redeemed for payout.
- Shares are transferable, so users can exit or reposition before final settlement.

## Settlement Types

Zedkr Market supports:

- **Event** markets — settle from verified real-world outcomes (admin review).
- **Price** markets — settle from oracle asset prices at resolve.
- **Token** markets — settle from DEX pool stats (Dexscreener / GeckoTerminal).
- **RWA (Prism)** markets — settle from Prism catalogue assets (price, mcap, APY).

## Design Goals

- Clear and simple participation flow.
- Mobile-friendly navigation with fast access to key actions.
- Transparent outcome and redemption behavior.
- Fair market mechanics that avoid hidden complexity for users.
